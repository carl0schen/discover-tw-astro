---
title: "Can I do a day trip from Taipei to southern cities like Kaohsiung or Tainan?"
order: 7
---

While technically possible via the Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR), a day trip from Taipei to southern Taiwan can be quite rushed. The journey takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours each way, depending on your destination and train service. You also need to account for additional travel time between the high-speed rail stations and local attractions.

For a more relaxed experience, spending at least one night in Tainan or Kaohsiung is recommended. This gives you more time to explore local attractions, enjoy regional cuisine, and experience the local culture without rushing.

If you are based in Taipei and prefer single-day excursions, consider closer destinations such as Jiufen, Yilan, or Taichung.
